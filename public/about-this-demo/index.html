About this demonstration

What this site is, and what it would take to make it the real one.

This is a working demonstration of a new website for the Grand Encampment of Knights Templar of the U.S.A. It is not an official Grand Encampment site. Every page is built from content the Grand Encampment already publishes on usagekt.org, so you can judge the design with your own words, officers and documents in it.

What is different

  • It keeps itself current. Each day it reads the usagekt.org calendar, State News and notices and rebuilds. Nobody has to post anything twice.
  • A calendar you can subscribe to. Events, including dates announced in notices and on the Department Conferences page, are in one list and in a feed that Google, Apple and Outlook calendars can follow.
  • State News by state. Every Grand Commandery's supplements in one searchable table, with the archive back to 2020.
  • Fast and easy to read. Plain pages with no page builder, readable on a phone, in light or dark mode, and built to meet accessibility standards (WCAG 2.2 AA).
  • Low cost and secure. Static pages served by Cloudflare: nothing to patch and nothing to hack.

What stays the same

Members' logins, forms, documents and the magazine stay where they are today. The site links to them; nothing is moved.
