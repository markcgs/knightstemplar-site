The 40 Mile Project

Ask the Grand Prelate for prayer for yourself or someone you love.

40 Miles Project - Contact Form

The purpose of the 40 Mile project is to provide a means whereby someone can reach out for contact or prayer for themselves or someone else when he knows that person is suffering because of illness, or loss. By filling out this contact form it will give the prelates the ability to communicate with you, and them. We, the Prelates, need to know both your information, and the information of the person you’re telling us about. Yours so that we can follow up, and theirs so we know who were praying for, and/or sending a card to.
SK Paul Erickson, Associate Grand Prelate, at agp@knightstemplar.org.

Instructions: Fill out all required fields and click “Lift up in Prayer” at the bottom.
