Grand Encampment of Knights Templar of the U.S.A.

Every Christian Mason should be a Knight Templar.

The Grand Encampment gives every Christian Freemason the opportunity to extend his Masonic journey through the chivalric experience: in the Commandery, in service to its charities, and in fellowship with Sir Knights across the world.
